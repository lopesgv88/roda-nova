FAQ

Common questions

How long does a bathroom remodel take?

Most full bathroom remodels take 2 to 4 weeks, depending on scope and material lead times. We give you a firm schedule in your estimate.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. We visit your home, review your goals, and send a detailed written quote at no cost.

Are you licensed and insured in Massachusetts?

Yes. We are a registered Home Improvement Contractor and carry full liability insurance. License numbers are available on request.

Can you install tile I buy myself?

Absolutely. We can install tile you've purchased, or help you choose materials. Just let us know in advance so we can plan the prep.

Do you handle permits?

Yes. When your project requires permits, we take care of the process with your local building department.
